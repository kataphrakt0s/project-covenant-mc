# Farming Profession

## Identity

Farming develops cultivation, harvesting, agricultural production, and potentially animal husbandry. It should add new ways to interact with Minecraft's crop and farming mechanics without gating ordinary crops, farmland, seeds, animals, or baseline farming.

## Established design direction

Farming may provide regenerative and sustain-oriented benefits, including passive, conditional, or active effects. Examples may relate to health regeneration, healing, food, or agricultural preparation, but exact abilities and effect conditions remain open.

Profession-based combat/survival effects are class-agnostic. All three classes can develop Farming. Benefits should be modest and complementary to combat skills and equipment.

## Progression integration

Farming starts at Level 0, defaults to a maximum of 20, and earns one Profession Point per level gained. It shares the common profession XP curve but has independent XP and points. Abilities are level-gated and purchased with variable Profession Point costs; selected milestones can automatically unlock core recipes.

Farming XP rewards should focus on meaningful actions such as harvesting mature crops and designated agricultural or livestock interactions, rather than repeated planting/breaking loops. Automated farms award no XP by default; configured exceptions require attribution and anti-exploit safeguards.

## Cross-profession and vanilla boundaries

Farming products can support optional Mining, Logging, or Crafting recipes. Specific cross-profession recipes may require levels or materials from multiple professions, but normal Farming abilities do not require other profession levels. Vanilla farming remains fully usable at profession level 0.

## Vertical-slice scope and open design

Include a functional progression track and a small playable selection of abilities/recipes. Exact agricultural systems, animal-husbandry scope, regeneration/healing bonuses, XP sources/values, point costs, level gates, and recipe milestones remain to be designed.
