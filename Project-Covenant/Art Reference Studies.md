# Art reference studies

## Direction reset

The user's current assessment: most Covenant art is too desaturated and realistic,
with insufficient bold dark fantasy character. **The wolf and traps are accepted
anchors. Everything else is eligible for eventual visual replacement.** This is a
reference and direction pass; it does not schedule an immediate wholesale redraw.

The first proposed combination, Minecraft Dungeons for objects plus Faithless for
icons, did not establish a direction. The user requested further exploration.
FurfSky now has selective approval as a reference, recorded below. No complete
external pack style or exact production palette is approved.

Review board: [local visual comparison](../.local/art-direction-2026-11-10/board.html).
The board and downloaded public previews are ignored local study material, absent
from fresh checkouts. The source links and decisions in this note are durable.

## FurfSky feedback — 2026-11-10

- **Accepted:** strong, distinctive silhouettes. This is the clearest useful
  quality and should inform original Covenant icon shapes.
- **Favoured colour treatments:** the darker examples using blacks, purples,
  reds and greys. These colour families are a direction, not fixed swatches.
- **Selective use only:** much of the collection is too flashy or neo-fantasy
  styled for Covenant's dark, gothic identity. Do not adopt the whole pack's look.
- **Production interpretation to test:** combine expressive silhouettes with
  rich dark colours, readable grey material planes and focused highlights.
  Preserve the earlier rejection of washed-out, overly realistic art; darker
  colours do not mean removing colour or obscuring the object in near-black.

## Gothic and occult combination — 2026-11-10

The user approved a contextual combination of forged weapons, armour and
cathedral ironwork with cursed relics, ritual objects and occult symbols.
Use gothic construction for physical form and occult details for meaning where
appropriate. The balance varies by asset; no equal mix or universal rune motif
is required. See [[Art Direction]] for working applications by asset purpose.

The user subsequently selected occult crimson fire, pale blue ice and vivid
electric blue lightning. Those elemental colour families are established;
exact per-asset shade ramps remain authored choices for review. A first runtime
implementation is available in the [before/after review](../.local/gothic-rework/review.html).

Future reference comparisons should pair useful construction examples with
relevant occult motifs. Exact ornament, material ramps and elemental exceptions
remain open; this agreement does not approve a whole reference pack.

## Base Minecraft study set

Inspect the resolved **Java 26.1.2** assets when preparing production briefs. A web
showcase or a Bedrock sample is not an exact version reference. Existing local
[vanilla item study](../.local/phase4/vanilla-study.png) is useful for comparing
blade, horn, amethyst, fire charge and blaze-powder silhouettes.

| Exact objects to compare | Question for the study | Candidate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Echo shard, recovery compass, ender pearl | How much coloured area does a dark object need to stay recognisable? | Occult components, cooldown/resource motifs. See Mojang's [Echo Shard article](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/echo-shard). |
| Enchanting table, brewing stand, respawn anchor | How do base, focal object and accent materials establish purpose? | Workbench: a recognisable ritual apparatus with a focal feature. |
| Amethyst shard, blaze powder, fire charge, prismarine crystals | How do silhouette and clustered highlights distinguish magic/materials? | Spell icons, projectile cores and elemental components. |
| Nether wart, crimson/warped materials, crying obsidian, soul fire | How can supernatural materials carry rich colour beside dark surfaces? | Colour studies for stone, bindings, elemental effects and future summons. |
| Existing Covenant wolf and four traps | Which proportions, pixel clusters and construction already work? | Preserve these assets; test new families beside them. |

These are proposed object studies, not claims that every named raw texture was
inspected in this pass. The local wolf, workbench, vanilla item sheet, current
skill/emblem sheet and component sheet were visually inspected.

## Asset-family decisions to make after choosing references

| Family | Current issue / review target | First comparison sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skills and class emblems | Thin symbols, repeated blades/gems and pale colours can weaken identity. | Cleave, Piercing Shot and Arcane Bolt at actual display size. |
| Status/aura icons | Shared identity must still distinguish action, buff and debuff. | One skill, its related aura and one status together. |
| Profession/navigation icons | Strong tools, crops and craft objects; cohesive with combat art. | Mining, Farming, Crafting and their menu frames. |
| Affix components/respecs | Current components share a pale framed-token treatment with small interior marks. | Health, attack damage and cooldown, plus both respec items. |
| Workbench model/atlas | Current grey top motif blends into surrounding stone; silhouette has little ritual specificity. | Existing one-block object beside enchanting table and selected fantasy prop references. |
| Spell models/textures/VFX | Agree on core shape, colour area and motion per element. | Fire, Ice, Lightning and Arcane at equivalent brightness/readability. |
| UI frames/HUD | Give colour and ornament a consistent supporting role. | One skill panel plus selected, locked and pending states. Keep functional resource colours recognisable. |
| Future golem/promotional models | Strong family shapes and material separation at Minecraft scale. | One neutral blockout and texture study before variants or poses. |
| Wolf/traps | Accepted anchors. | Comparison context; no redesign from this brief. |

## Review method

1. Pick a specific reference image and name the quality that works: shape,
   colour, material, motif or animation. A whole pack need not be approved.
2. Agree on one small family sample before extending the look across the mod.
3. Compare at native size and integer enlargement, on light and dark backgrounds,
   beside the accepted wolf/traps and vanilla assets. Colour and shape both matter.
4. Record accepted/rejected qualities here. Unanswered choices remain open.

Study links are not production assets. Keep creator attribution with previews;
author original Covenant assets rather than copying or recolouring these sheets.
Existing source collection remains intact at
`docs/art/references/covenant_gothic_lite_references/`.
