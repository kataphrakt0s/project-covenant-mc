# Enemy Progression

Enemy difficulty reflects region, enemy role, and encounter context. Avoid universal player-level scaling that removes world identity.

## Initial progression-slice XP sources (planned)

Hostile mobs grant character XP by default. Other combat mobs can be explicitly
included through entity IDs and tags; neutral mobs require that inclusion.
Include vanilla Endermen explicitly as the reference for the agreed final-level
target: the starting Level 59-to-60 benchmark is 3,000 ordinary vanilla Enderman
kills or 30 reference boss kills at full solo rewards where the dimension gate
permits Level 60. The reference is a repeat Wither kill at 2,000 base character
XP (100 ordinary Endermen), before sharing/caps. Under the default route, the
final-level benchmark requires repeat Wither kills in the End; repeat kills in
the Overworld/Nether obey their 20/40 caps. The first-time level milestone is
separate and replaces that recipient's ordinary XP share. Other boss rewards
remain open. Ordinary
livestock and player kills grant no character XP in
the default slice. This reward eligibility is separate from ordinary combat and
skill targeting. Reward amounts and the concrete authored entries remain open.
See [[Experience and Reward Systems]] and [[Progression Balance Configuration]].

To design: enemy stats, difficulty bands, elite/champion modifiers, exact XP/rewards, and additional modded entity content. Multiplayer attribution follows [[Experience and Reward Systems]].
