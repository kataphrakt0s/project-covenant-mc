# Statistics

## Direct statistics

The current direct-stat vocabulary is:

- Health
- Damage
- Armor
- Movement Speed
- Attack Speed
- Dodge

Skill-related modifier channels include Resource Cost, Cooldown, Cast Time, and Skill Duration.

## Progression sources

There is no traditional primary-attribute system and no attribute-point allocation. Character levels award combat skill points rather than direct stat points. General numerical character growth comes primarily from equipped items and [[Affix Pages]], with skill effects and deliberately modest, class-agnostic profession effects providing additional build variation.

Vanilla and modded equipment should remain relevant. Covenant should avoid uncontrolled stat inflation that makes normal Minecraft equipment or encounters obsolete.

Use Minecraft attributes and native equipment modifiers where appropriate. Permanent character state remains separate from ItemStack-owned Affix Pages and effective equipment values.

See [the implementation contract](../docs/RPG_STATS.md) for current stat IDs, units, defaults, and limits. Additional modifier sources must be introduced when a concrete gameplay requirement justifies them.
