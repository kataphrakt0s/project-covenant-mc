# Milestone 1 — RPG Foundation

## Goal
Persistent character progression, class resources, item-bound Affix Pages, and reliable stat foundation.

## Scope
Player profiles; leveling/XP; class definitions/resources; initial attributes; Affix Pages on stacks; equipment category resolution; equipped-item discovery; stat calculation pipeline; debug commands and tests.

## Acceptance proof
A player creates and levels a character, equips an existing Minecraft item with an Affix Page, receives its modifiers while equipped, unequips without losing the page, transfers it to another player, and retains it after save/reload.

## Out of scope
Full UI, talent trees, complete combat abilities, affix generation, crafting interface, world generation, mobs, bosses, endgame.
