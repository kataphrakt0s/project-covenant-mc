# Core Gameplay Loop

Explore and participate in Minecraft's world → fight, mine, log, farm, craft, and explore → earn separate Covenant character XP and activity-specific profession XP → develop a combat build and practical profession mastery → gather and craft equipment → tune gear with Affix Pages and future workstations → tackle more dangerous dimensions, encounters, dungeons, and bosses → repeat with new build and mastery goals.

Character progression is primarily combat-driven. Mining, Logging, Farming, and Crafting each have independent progression. Vanilla XP orbs remain separate. Profession progression primarily unlocks new capabilities and interactions; it does not gate baseline vanilla gameplay.

Short term: meaningful encounters and gathering. Medium term: class skills, profession abilities/recipes, equipment and crafting. Long term: dimension mastery, refined builds, profession specializations, dungeons, bosses, world events, and gear customization.
