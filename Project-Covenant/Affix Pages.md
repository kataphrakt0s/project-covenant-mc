# Affix Pages — Retired Concept

The standalone **Affix Page** item concept has been retired and is no longer part of the current design.

Covenant affixes are properties stored directly on individual equipment items. Players add, upgrade, reroll, and remove those affixes through the Covenant Workbench. Affixed equipment remains tradeable; there are no standalone pages to collect, trade, apply, or salvage.

See [[Affix System and Equipment Customization]] for the current design and [[Salvaging]] for the component recovery rules.
