# Crafting

Crafting remains part of Minecraft's normal gameplay. Covenant must not gate baseline vanilla crafting, stations, recipes, or ordinary enchanting behind profession levels.

## Crafting profession

The Crafting profession is one of the four initial professions. Its dedicated
prototype branches are affix crafting and salvaging. Successful vanilla
enchanting is a supported Crafting XP activity, not a separate Covenant
specialization or replacement enchanting system. Ordinary vanilla crafting
does not award Crafting XP. See [[Crafting Profession]] and [[Profession Progression]].

## Equipment creation and customization

Covenant's equipment philosophy is to enhance existing vanilla and modded items rather than require a separate replacement gear ecosystem. Players should eventually be able to create, modify, tune, and salvage equipment through specialized Covenant systems.

The prototype uses one physical Covenant Workbench with separate Affix Crafting
and Salvaging interfaces and persistent shared storage. Their agreed interaction
and cost rules are recorded in [[Crafting Profession]] and [[Salvaging]];
remaining recipes, material tables, and balance values are still under design.
These are implemented prototype systems; see [Itemization](../docs/ITEMIZATION.md). Vanilla
crafting stations continue to function normally.

The agreed Workbench prototype recipe is **shapeless** in a vanilla crafting
table: **one crafting table, four planks, two iron ingots, and two lapis
lazuli**, producing one Workbench. Any/mixed plank types qualify. Crafting the
station has no profession requirement and grants no profession XP; the
operations themselves require their purchased profession unlocks.

## Cross-profession recipes

Most profession advancement and abilities do not require levels in another
profession. Future specific cross-profession recipes may intentionally require
multiple profession levels or outputs as explicit recipe-level exceptions,
not a general dependency rule. **Prototype Profession Respec has no
profession-level or purchased-ability crafting requirement**. Craft one using
a **shapeless crafting-table recipe of four paper, four lapis lazuli, and one
emerald**, exceeding Combat Respec's material cost. This ordinary recipe
grants no profession XP. For the prototype, one item refunds **all four
professions' allocations together**, retaining their XP/levels. Use it like
Combat Respec: hold right-click for two seconds out of combat, consuming the
item only when the server completes the reset. See [[Profession Progression]].
