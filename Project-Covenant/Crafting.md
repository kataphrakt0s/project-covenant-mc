# Crafting

Crafting remains part of Minecraft's normal gameplay. Covenant must not gate baseline vanilla crafting, stations, recipes, or ordinary enchanting behind profession levels.

## Crafting profession

The Crafting profession is one of the four initial professions. Enchanting is a subset/specialization within Crafting, alongside advanced equipment crafting, affix manipulation, and salvaging. Ordinary vanilla crafting does not award Crafting XP; meaningful designated activities do. See [[Crafting Profession]] and [[Profession Progression]].

## Equipment creation and customization

Covenant's equipment philosophy is to enhance existing vanilla and modded items rather than require a separate replacement gear ecosystem. Players should eventually be able to create, modify, tune, and salvage equipment through specialized Covenant systems.

Covenant-specific workstations are planned for advanced affix crafting and salvaging. Their exact roster, interactions, recipes, materials, and implementation milestone remain undecided. Vanilla crafting stations continue to function normally.

## Cross-profession recipes

Most profession advancement and abilities do not require levels in another profession. Specific cross-profession recipes may intentionally require multiple profession levels or outputs. This is an explicit recipe-level exception, not a general dependency rule. The proposed Profession Respec recipe requiring level 10 in all four professions is an example under consideration, not a finalized recipe.
