# Design Pillars

1. **Minecraft-native progression:** Reward combat and the many activities that make Minecraft a sandbox. Do not gate baseline vanilla functionality behind Covenant levels.
2. **Meaningful character builds:** Character levels support deliberate class-skill investment, with an eight-skill allocation limit and meaningful rank choices.
3. **Profession mastery:** Mining, Logging, Farming, and Crafting provide independent, class-agnostic paths that primarily unlock new capabilities and interactions.
4. **Meaningful itemization and player agency:** Existing vanilla and modded equipment remains relevant; Affix Pages and future crafting/salvaging systems let players tune gear.
5. **Hybrid Minecraft/RPG combat:** Add class identity and tactical abilities while respecting familiar Minecraft weapons, equipment, and encounters.
6. **Mod compatibility:** Use identifiers, tags, data-driven definitions, and optional integrations instead of mandatory dependencies or hardcoded support lists.
7. **Sustainable long-term play:** Dimensions, dungeons, bosses, professions, gear customization, and repeatable challenges provide goals within the level caps.
