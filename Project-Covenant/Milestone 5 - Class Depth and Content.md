# Milestone 5 — Class Depth and Content

Expanded class skills/ranks and build options; the vertical-slice profession ability/recipe selections for Mining, Logging, Farming, and Crafting; optional cross-profession recipes; profession specializations and their mutually exclusive alternatives; expanded enemy archetypes and encounter mechanics; additional regions, dungeons, bosses; optional compatibility integrations.

The initial vertical slice includes all four professions with a deliberately modest but playable selection, not fully populated trees.
