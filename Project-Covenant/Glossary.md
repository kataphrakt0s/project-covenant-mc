# Glossary

- **Affix:** Item modifier changing RPG properties.
- **Affix Page:** Item-bound RPG data attached to an individual Minecraft stack.
- **Character XP:** Covenant experience earned primarily through combat and significant milestones; separate from vanilla XP orbs.
- **Character skill point:** Point earned from character levels and spent on the class skill tree.
- **Combat skill allocation:** A skill with at least one point invested; no more than eight distinct skills may be allocated simultaneously.
- **Dimension gate:** Configurable maximum level for a progression track based on the current dimension.
- **Profession:** An independent activity progression track: Mining, Logging, Farming, or Crafting.
- **Profession Point:** Point earned when a profession gains a level, spent to purchase profession abilities.
- **Profession XP:** Activity-specific Covenant XP; independent for each profession and separate from vanilla XP orbs.
- **Specialization:** A focused branch or mutually exclusive option within a skill/profession tree.
- **Vanilla XP:** Minecraft's normal experience-orb and enchanting-level system; it does not grant Covenant progression.
- **Contributor XP:** Combat XP divided among players who qualify as contributors to the encounter; exact qualification and share formula remain to be finalized.
- **Derived statistic:** Calculated stat based on base values and applicable modifier sources.
