# Endgame

The character level cap is **60**. There is no level-100 or post-level-100 character progression track in the current design.

Long-term progression comes from:

- Reaching and mastering later dimensions and their encounters.
- Completing dungeons, bosses, world events, and challenge tiers.
- Refining class builds through skill ranks and the eight-skill allocation limit.
- Developing Mining, Logging, Farming, and Crafting toward their profession caps.
- Acquiring and tuning equipment through Affix Pages, crafting, and salvaging.
- Pursuing optional cross-profession recipes and multiplayer trade/cooperation.

Endgame should reward build mastery, world knowledge, equipment tuning, and meaningful activities rather than raising the character level cap indefinitely. Avoid indiscriminate enemy scaling based solely on player level.
